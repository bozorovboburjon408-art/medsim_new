1. ELEKTRONIKA VA DETALLARNI ULASH SXEMASI (HARDWARE PINOUT)
================================================================================

KERAKLI MODULLAR:
1. ESP32 DevKit V1 (30-pin yoki 38-pin)
2. PAM8403 Ovoz kuchaytirgich moduli (2x3W) va 3W-5W Dinamik (Kalonka)
3. MPU-6050 (GY-521) Akselerometr + Giroskop sensori (Chaqaloq manikeni uchun)
4. Ulash simlari (Jumper wires)
5. 5V quvvat manbai (Micro-USB yoki Type-C)

SIMLARNI ULASH JADVALI:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32 PIN            MODUL PIN              VAZIFASI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GPIO 25 (DAC1)  ---> PAM8403 L_IN           Chap kanal audio signali (Ichki DAC)
GPIO 26 (DAC2)  ---> PAM8403 R_IN           O'ng kanal audio signali (Ichki DAC)
GND             ---> PAM8403 GND            Umumiy yer (massa)
5V (yoki VIN)   ---> PAM8403 +5V            Kuchaytirgich quvvati
GPIO 21         ---> MPU-6050 SDA           I2C Ma'lumot signali (Data)
GPIO 22         ---> MPU-6050 SCL           I2C Soat signali (Clock)
3.3V (yoki 5V)  ---> MPU-6050 VCC           Sensor quvvati
GND             ---> MPU-6050 GND           Sensor yeri
GPIO 2 (Ichki)  ---> LED                    Holat indikatori
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
